# Build your resume page

Hello there !!
For this assignment or task, we expect you to design a single webpage, showcasing your ability to design a page. the page should not be too verbose. 

## Just require...
1. your image on the page. (must be positioned in a likable fashion...)
2. About yourself...
3. Your Hobbies....
4. Also a fun thing about yourself....
5. Also, your most loved project (by you)...
6. Also, your most loved quotes in the internet (the good ones) ...


We expect you to use `React` and one of `Bootstrap` or `Tailwind`. for this project. We expect you people to understand the crux of designing. Also, you can use any `components` from internet
## Goal:
    We want to know, how creative you can be. Thats it. !!!
